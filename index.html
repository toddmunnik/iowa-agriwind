Where the numbers come from. Cash rents, yields and production costs: Iowa State University Extension and Outreach, Ag Decision Maker files C2-10 and A1-20, 2026. The $4,000 per MW starting lease: Iowa State University Center for Agricultural and Rural Development (CARD), Agricultural Policy Review. Wind: Global Wind Atlas 3.0, a free, web-based application developed, owned and operated by the Technical University of Denmark (DTU), released in partnership with the World Bank Group (ESMAP) using data provided by Vortex, licensed CC BY 4.0: globalwindatlas.info. Land taken by turbines: NREL, Denholm et al., 2009, Land-Use Requirements of Modern Wind Power Plants in the United States. County center points: US Census Bureau 2026 Gazetteer.

How to read this. Figures are returns per acre per year over variable costs, before land, machinery ownership and labor. The wind lease per acre is the lease per MW times the MW of turbines per acre at the chosen spacing. Land under turbine pads and roads is removed from farming, and the rest is farmed with the same formula as farm-only: crop yield × price, minus input costs. Wake losses use a simple spacing rule. This is a planning estimate, not a lease offer, an engineering study or financial advice. County zoning, setbacks, moratoriums and the property tax treatment of wind are not modeled.
